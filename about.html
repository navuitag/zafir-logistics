About ZAFIR Logistics

Connecting Vietnam to Africa & global markets with reliability and excellence

Who We Are

ZAFIR Logistics Vietnam is a dynamic international logistics provider headquartered in Ho Chi Minh City, specializing in shipments between Vietnam and Africa.

With strong expertise in ocean freight, project cargo, and African trade lanes, we help Vietnamese exporters reliably reach key African markets such as Abidjan, Tema, and Lomé.
