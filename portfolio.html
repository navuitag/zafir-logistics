Core Services Portfolio

End-to-end capabilities aligned with how global shippers move cargo from Vietnam.

Ocean Freight

Seamless container management across major maritime trade lanes. We navigate complex shipping schedules so your cargo moves without friction across the global blue economy.

Air Freight

Rapid, time-definite aerial logistics for high-priority shipments. We bridge Vietnamese manufacturing hubs and international markets with maximum velocity.

Breakbulk

Specialized handling and individualized stowage for non-containerized cargo. We manage technical loading for heavy or irregularly shaped goods with expert precision.

Project Cargo

End-to-end management of out-of-gauge (OOG) and industrial oversized equipment. Heavy-lift transport for massive machinery and infrastructure components.

Logistics Solutions

Integrated warehousing and supply chain optimization. From inventory to final-mile distribution, we streamline operations to cut overhead and lift efficiency.
