Vietnam – Africa Trade Routes

We specialize in reliable and cost-effective shipping between Vietnam and key African markets.

Our Main Trade Lanes

🇨🇮

Abidjan, Ivory Coast

West Africa’s Commercial Hub

Major economic center and busiest port in West Africa. Ideal for general cargo, machinery, and consumer goods.

Transit Time 35 – 42 days
Frequency Weekly sailings
🇬🇭

Tema, Ghana

Modern & Fast-Growing Port

One of the most modern ports in West Africa with excellent infrastructure. Popular for industrial equipment and project cargo.

Transit Time 36 – 43 days
Frequency Weekly sailings
🇹🇬

Lomé, Togo

Strategic Transshipment Hub

Important regional hub with excellent connectivity to neighboring countries. Great for distribution across West Africa.

Transit Time 34 – 41 days
Frequency Bi-weekly sailings
🇮🇳

Nhava Sheva (Mumbai), India

South Asia’s Major Gateway

India’s busiest container port and primary gateway for subcontinent trade. Suited to general cargo, textiles, and industrial supplies.

Transit Time 12 – 18 days
Frequency Weekly sailings

Domestic — Vietnam

Nationwide Port & Inland Network

Coastal feeders, barges, and trucking linking Cat Lai, Cái Mép, Hai Phong, Da Nang, and key industrial zones across Vietnam.

Transit Time 2 – 6 days
Frequency Daily / scheduled
